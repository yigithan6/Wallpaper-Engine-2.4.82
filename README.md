# Wallpaper Engine

Wallpaper Engine is a program for Windows that allows you to use live wallpapers on your desktop. You can choose from various types of animated wallpapers, including 3D and 2D animations, websites, videos, and even some apps. You can also create your own wallpapers and share them on Steam Workshop!

## Features

### 1. **Optimized for Performance**
The program automatically pauses the wallpapers when playing games or using fullscreen applications to ensure high system performance.

### 2. **Supports All Resolutions and Aspect Ratios**
Wallpaper Engine supports all monitor resolutions and aspect ratios, including ultra-wide resolutions. It can even work with complex multi-monitor setups.

### 3. **Wide Selection of Wallpapers**
You can create your own wallpapers and share them with the community or use one of the hundreds of thousands of wallpapers available for free on Steam Workshop.

### 4. **Customizable Settings**
You can adjust various performance options and set rules for launching specific applications. On laptops, the program can automatically pause the wallpapers when switching to battery power.

### 5. **Playlists and Slideshow**
You can create playlists that change your wallpapers at specified intervals, based on the time of day, when you log in, or when you launch a specific application on your computer.

### 6. **LED Hardware Support**
The program can control Corsair iCUE and Razer Chroma RGB LED hardware and synchronize the lighting with your wallpapers.

### 7. **Free Mobile App**
You can use the free Wallpaper Engine mobile app for Android to wirelessly transfer your favorite wallpapers to your Android phone or tablet and enjoy your wallpaper collection on the go.
